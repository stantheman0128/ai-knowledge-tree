## Superhuman faces scrutiny over AI impersonation capabilities

The CEO of Superhuman was confronted regarding the company's AI email assistant, which drafts replies so convincingly that recipients cannot distinguish between human and AI authorship. This highlights the growing ethical concerns regarding AI-driven identity and the lack of disclosure standards for AI-composed communications.
