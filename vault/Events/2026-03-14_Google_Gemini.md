## Gemini gains real-world task automation on Android devices

Google has updated Gemini on Samsung Galaxy S26 and Pixel devices to function as a transaction agent capable of ordering food and rideshares. The system operates as a cross-app automation layer, marking a shift from simple chatbots to autonomous agents that execute real-world tasks.
