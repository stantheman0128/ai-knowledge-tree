## Study reveals surface cues systematically hijack LLM reasoning

A new study demonstrates that LLMs prioritize surface-level keywords over implicit constraints by a factor of 8-38x. This pattern-matching behavior suggests models often act as keyword matchers rather than logical reasoners, though performance can be improved with targeted prompting hints.
