## OpenAI rotates macOS certificates following axios supply chain attack

OpenAI rotated its macOS code signing certificates after a compromised version of the axios library was identified in its toolchain. While no user data was compromised, the incident highlights the risks of supply chain vulnerabilities for AI companies marketing HIPAA-compliant products to healthcare providers.
