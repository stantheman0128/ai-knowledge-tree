## RoboAlign improves robot manipulation through inference-time reasoning alignment.

RoboAlign shifts reasoning supervision from training to inference time, using RL to align sampled action tokens with natural language reasoning. This approach significantly improves performance in real-world robotic tasks while requiring minimal data post-SFT.
