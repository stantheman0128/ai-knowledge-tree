## OpenAI proposes a new industrial policy framework for the AI era

OpenAI has released a policy paper outlining a people-first industrial strategy designed to maximize the benefits of advanced intelligence. The proposal focuses on expanding economic opportunities, ensuring shared prosperity, and strengthening institutional resilience as AI technology continues to evolve.
