## IndexCache eliminates 75% of sparse attention indexer compute through cross-layer reuse

IndexCache optimizes sparse attention by reusing indices across adjacent layers, which share high overlap in token selection. This engineering optimization provides a 1.82x prefill speedup on 30B models with negligible quality loss.
