## Apple to allow third-party AI chatbots to integrate with Siri in iOS 27

Apple is opening Siri to third-party AI models, allowing users to select chatbots like Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT to handle requests. This strategic shift acknowledges Apple's current gap in AI model performance while maintaining its control over the iOS interface.
