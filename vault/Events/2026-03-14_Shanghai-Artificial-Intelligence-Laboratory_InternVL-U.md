## Shanghai AI Lab releases InternVL-U unified multimodal model

The lab launched InternVL-U, a 4B parameter model that integrates multimodal understanding, reasoning, and image generation into a single framework. The release includes open-source weights and new evaluation benchmarks for text-based image editing.
