## TII open-sources Falcon Perception and Falcon OCR models

The Abu Dhabi-based TII has released two lightweight vision models: Falcon Perception for open-vocabulary segmentation and Falcon OCR for document understanding. These models offer high performance and efficiency for specialized visual tasks.
