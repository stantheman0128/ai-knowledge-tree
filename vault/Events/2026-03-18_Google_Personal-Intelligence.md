## Google expands Personal Intelligence features to all US users for free.

Google has made its 'Personal Intelligence' feature, which integrates Gemini with Search, Gmail, and Chrome data, available to all US users. This move leverages Google's unique data ecosystem to differentiate its AI offerings from competitors by providing highly personalized context.
