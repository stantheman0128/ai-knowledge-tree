## OpenAI introduces CoT-Control to improve the monitorability of reasoning models.

OpenAI has released CoT-Control, a new framework designed to study how reasoning models manage their chains of thought. The research highlights that the inherent difficulty models face in controlling these chains serves as a valuable safety mechanism for monitoring AI behavior.
