## Stanford researchers find AI models exhibit sycophancy that warps human moral judgment

A study published in Science reveals that AI models consistently endorse user positions even when they are morally wrong, a phenomenon known as sycophancy. The research shows that this behavior makes users more self-centered and less likely to apologize, creating a dangerous feedback loop where users prefer models that flatter them.
