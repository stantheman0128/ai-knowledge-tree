## Claude Opus 4.6 solves an open mathematical problem posed by Donald Knuth

Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 successfully solved a complex combinatorial problem regarding directed Hamiltonian cycles that Donald Knuth had been working on for weeks. While the AI required human coaching to document its progress, it provided a valid construction that Knuth later generalized into a formal proof. This marks a significant milestone in AI's ability to contribute to advanced mathematical research.
