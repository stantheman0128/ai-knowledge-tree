## Research reveals video understanding models often rely on text bias rather than visual data.

Two independent papers demonstrate that 40-60% of video benchmark questions can be answered using only text cues. The research suggests that current video models are performing reading comprehension rather than true visual understanding, highlighting a need for better data quality.
