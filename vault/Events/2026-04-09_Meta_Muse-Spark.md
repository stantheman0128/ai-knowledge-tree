## Meta releases Muse Spark, a new multimodal reasoning model

Meta Superintelligence Labs has launched Muse Spark, its first native multimodal reasoning model built on a reconstructed tech stack. The model excels in visual and scientific tasks and is currently integrated into Meta's social platforms and available via private API.
