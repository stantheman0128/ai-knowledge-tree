## Zhipu AI releases GLM-5V-Turbo multimodal model for visual programming

Zhipu AI has launched GLM-5V-Turbo, a multimodal model featuring a 200K context window and native support for image and video inputs. It is designed to excel in agentic workflows, specifically for visual programming and GUI task automation.
