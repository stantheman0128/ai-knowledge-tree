## CodePercept demonstrates that STEM visual reasoning bottlenecks are due to perception, not reasoning.

Research shows that scaling the perception component of MLLMs yields better results than scaling reasoning. The team introduced CodePercept, which uses executable code to parse visual information, improving performance on STEM tasks.
