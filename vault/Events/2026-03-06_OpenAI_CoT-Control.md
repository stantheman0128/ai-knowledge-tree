## OpenAI open-sources CoT-Control evaluation suite for reasoning models

OpenAI released the CoT-Control evaluation suite to measure the controllability of reasoning chains in frontier models. The research suggests that current models lack the ability to manipulate their own reasoning, which the company frames as a safety feature.
