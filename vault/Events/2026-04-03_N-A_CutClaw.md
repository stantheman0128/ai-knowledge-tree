## CutClaw uses multi-agent collaboration for long-form video editing

CutClaw decomposes the video editing process into three specialized agents: narrative planning, shot selection, and quality review. This approach enables the automated editing of hours-long footage into music-synced clips, focusing on practical creator workflows.
