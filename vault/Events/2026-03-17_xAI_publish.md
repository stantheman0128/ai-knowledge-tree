## xAI researchers propose minimum-size abductive explanations for model rejections

Researchers at xAI developed a method to explain model rejections by identifying the minimum set of features that caused the decision. By framing the problem as 0-1 integer linear programming, they provide more actionable insights for high-stakes human-AI collaboration.
