## Google begins replacing publisher headlines with AI-generated versions in search results

Google has started using AI to rewrite news headlines in search results, overriding the original headlines chosen by publishers. This change removes editorial control from publishers and lacks an opt-out mechanism, raising concerns about transparency and provenance.
