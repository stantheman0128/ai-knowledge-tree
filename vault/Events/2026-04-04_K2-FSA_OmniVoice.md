## Researchers release OmniVoice, a 600-language zero-shot TTS model

A research team has open-sourced OmniVoice, a large-scale text-to-speech model supporting over 600 languages. It utilizes a diffusion language model architecture to enable zero-shot voice cloning and instruction-based audio design.
