## Netflix develops custom AI models for film production workflows

Netflix is creating bespoke AI models designed to optimize specific technical tasks in post-production and visual effects. The initiative, involving filmmaker Ben Affleck, aims to streamline industrial film processes rather than replace creative authorship.
