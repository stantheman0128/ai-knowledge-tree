## Mistral releases Leanstral, an open-source agent for formal proof engineering.

Mistral has open-sourced Leanstral, a specialized model designed to assist with formal proof engineering in the Lean 4 language. This release signals a strategic shift toward purpose-built, domain-specific tools that prioritize ecosystem adoption over general-purpose performance.
