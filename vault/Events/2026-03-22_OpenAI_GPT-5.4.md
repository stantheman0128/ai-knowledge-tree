## OpenAI releases GPT-5.4 frontend design guidelines and frontend-skill

OpenAI has released new design guidelines for GPT-5.4, emphasizing visual references and optimized inference levels for frontend tasks. The company also introduced a 'frontend-skill' tool to help developers enforce design constraints and improve UI output quality.
