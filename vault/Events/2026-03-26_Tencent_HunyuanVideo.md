## SAGE-GRPO stabilizes video generation by preventing off-manifold exploration.

SAGE-GRPO addresses training instability in video generation by using manifold-projected exploration and dual trust regions. This prevents noise from pushing sampling trajectories off the pretrained data manifold, improving quality for models like HunyuanVideo.
