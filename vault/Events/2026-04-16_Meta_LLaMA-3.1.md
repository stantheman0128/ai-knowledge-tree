## LASA method improves multilingual safety alignment by targeting semantic bottleneck layers

Researchers introduced LASA, a technique that anchors safety alignment at the model's semantic bottleneck layer rather than surface-level English text. This approach significantly reduces attack success rates in low-resource languages for models like LLaMA-3.1.
