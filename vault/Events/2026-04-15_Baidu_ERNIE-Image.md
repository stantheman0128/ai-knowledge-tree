## Baidu open-sources ERNIE-Image model for text-to-image generation

Baidu has released its 8B parameter ERNIE-Image model, which utilizes a Diffusion Transformer architecture. It is designed for high-controllability tasks like poster and comic generation with support for long Chinese text rendering.
