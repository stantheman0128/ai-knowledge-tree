## AURA introduces end-to-end streaming architecture for real-time VideoLLM interaction.

AURA is a new framework that enables VideoLLMs to process live video streams continuously with proactive response capabilities. By unifying perception and generation, it achieves 2 FPS performance and eliminates the need for decoupled trigger-response pipelines.
