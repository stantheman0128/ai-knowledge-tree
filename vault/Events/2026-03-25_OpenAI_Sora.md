## OpenAI shuts down Sora video generator and cancels billion-dollar Disney deal

OpenAI has officially discontinued its Sora video generation model just 15 months after its launch. The decision includes the termination of a major $1 billion licensing partnership with Disney as the company pivots its focus toward enterprise productivity tools.
