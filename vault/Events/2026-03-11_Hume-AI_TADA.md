## Hume AI releases TADA, a speech-language unified model based on Llama 3.2.

Hume AI has open-sourced TADA, a model based on Llama 3.2 that aligns text and acoustic tokens. It supports both text-to-speech synthesis and speech continuation in 1B and 3B parameter sizes.
