## NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3 Super, a 120B parameter open model optimized for agentic AI workflows.

NVIDIA has launched Nemotron 3 Super, an open-weights model featuring a hybrid mixture-of-experts architecture designed to handle complex multi-agent tasks with 5x higher throughput. The model includes a 1-million-token context window and is released with full training datasets and recipes to support enterprise-scale agentic AI deployment.
