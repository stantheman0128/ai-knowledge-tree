## Microsoft AI releases MAI-Image-2 text-to-image model

Microsoft AI has introduced MAI-Image-2, a new image generation model featuring enhanced photorealism and improved text rendering. The model is currently available on MAI Playground and is rolling out to Copilot and Bing Image Creator.
