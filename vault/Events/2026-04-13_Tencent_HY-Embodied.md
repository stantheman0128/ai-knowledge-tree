## Tencent releases HY-Embodied, a VLM-based architecture for unified robot perception and planning.

Tencent has introduced HY-Embodied, a model that replaces traditional modular robotics pipelines with a single VLM for perception and planning. The release includes a 2B on-device model and a 32B reasoning model, aiming to simplify embodied AI architectures.
