## Nscale closes $2 billion funding round at $14.6 billion valuation

British AI infrastructure startup Nscale has secured $2 billion in new funding, bringing its valuation to $14.6 billion. The company, which counts Nvidia among its backers, has also appointed Sheryl Sandberg and Nick Clegg to its board to help expand its sovereign AI deals in Europe.
