## Grammarly criticized for using identities of experts without authorization

Grammarly launched an AI feature that used the names and reputations of subject matter experts, including deceased professors and living journalists, without their knowledge or consent. The incident underscores the lack of legal frameworks governing the use of personal identity and credentials in AI-generated content.
