## NVIDIA and Thinking Machines Lab announce a gigawatt-scale strategic partnership

NVIDIA has entered a multiyear partnership with Thinking Machines Lab to deploy gigawatt-scale Vera Rubin AI systems for frontier model training. The collaboration also includes a significant investment from NVIDIA to support the development of customizable AI platforms for enterprises and the scientific community.
