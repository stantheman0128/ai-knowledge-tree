## Allbirds pivots to AI infrastructure under the name Hyperscale

Following the sale of its footwear assets, the remaining corporate shell of Allbirds has rebranded as Hyperscale, claiming a pivot to AI data center operations. The announcement triggered a 600% stock surge despite the company disclosing no technical team, product roadmap, or revenue model.
