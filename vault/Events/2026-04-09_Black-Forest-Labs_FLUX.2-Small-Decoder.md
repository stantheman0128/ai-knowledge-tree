## Black Forest Labs releases open-source FLUX.2 Small Decoder

Black Forest Labs has introduced the FLUX.2 Small Decoder, which significantly improves decoding speed and reduces VRAM usage for the FLUX.2 model family. The decoder is released under the Apache 2.0 license, making it accessible for real-time applications.
