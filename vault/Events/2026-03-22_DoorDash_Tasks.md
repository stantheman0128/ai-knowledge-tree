## DoorDash launches Tasks app to convert gig workers into AI training data suppliers

DoorDash introduced a new app that pays delivery couriers to record videos and audio for AI training purposes. This initiative creates a new labor category where gig workers are repurposed as data suppliers for multimodal AI development.
