## Meta suspends relationship with data vendor Mercor following security breach

Meta has paused its partnership with data supplier Mercor after a security incident potentially exposed proprietary model training details. The breach highlights systemic vulnerabilities in the AI industry's reliance on third-party data vendors for sensitive training information.
