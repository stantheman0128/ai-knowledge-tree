## MoScale applies hierarchical next-scale autoregressive modeling to improve text-to-motion generation.

MoScale moves away from flattened 1D sequence generation for human motion, instead using a coarse-to-fine hierarchical approach. This method achieves state-of-the-art results on text-to-motion tasks and demonstrates strong zero-shot generalization for motion editing.
