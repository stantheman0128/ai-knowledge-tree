## DC-DiT optimizes diffusion model compute by allocating tokens based on information density

DC-DiT introduces a dynamic token allocation mechanism that compresses low-information regions and focuses compute on high-detail areas. The model adapts its strategy across denoising stages and can warm-start from existing DiT checkpoints to improve efficiency.
