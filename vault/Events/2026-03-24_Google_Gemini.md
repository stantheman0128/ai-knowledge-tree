## Google rolls out Gemini task automation for Pixel and Galaxy devices

Google has released new task automation features for Gemini on mobile devices, allowing the AI to interact directly with apps to perform actions like ordering food or hailing rides. While early reports note the feature is currently slow, it represents a significant step in on-device AI integration.
