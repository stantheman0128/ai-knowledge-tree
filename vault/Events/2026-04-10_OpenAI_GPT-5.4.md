## Latent planning in LLMs hits a hard depth ceiling

A study on GPT-5.4 and other models shows that latent planning is limited to 5-7 steps, which scaling does not overcome. This finding supports the necessity of externalizing reasoning through Chain-of-Thought for complex tasks.
