## Google releases Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS with natural language voice control

Google has launched a new text-to-speech model that allows developers to define voice characteristics through natural language prompts rather than traditional audio parameters. This shift simplifies voice application development by treating voice direction as a prompt engineering task, enabling more expressive output without specialized audio expertise.
