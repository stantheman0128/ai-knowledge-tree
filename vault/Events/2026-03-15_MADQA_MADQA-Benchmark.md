## New benchmark reveals multimodal agents rely on brute-force search rather than strategic reasoning

The MADQA benchmark evaluates multimodal agents on document-heavy tasks and finds they match human accuracy through random search rather than strategic planning. The study highlights a 20% performance gap compared to Oracle models, suggesting current agent architectures have significant structural weaknesses.
