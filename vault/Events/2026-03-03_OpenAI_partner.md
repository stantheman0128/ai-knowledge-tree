## OpenAI signs a controversial contract with the U.S. Department of Defense

OpenAI has entered into a deal with the Pentagon to provide AI technology for national security purposes. The agreement includes restrictions on autonomous weapons and mass surveillance, though critics note it lacks protections against the government's use of publicly available data.
