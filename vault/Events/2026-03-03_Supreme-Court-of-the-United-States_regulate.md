## Supreme Court denies copyright protection for AI-generated works

The U.S. Supreme Court declined to hear an appeal regarding whether AI-generated art can be copyrighted. This decision effectively upholds the requirement of human authorship for copyright protection, closing a major legal path for AI creators.
