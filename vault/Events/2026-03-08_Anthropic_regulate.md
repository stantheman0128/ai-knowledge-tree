## Pentagon labels Anthropic a supply-chain risk over policy disagreements

The U.S. Department of Defense has formally designated Anthropic as a supply-chain risk due to ongoing disputes regarding the company's acceptable use policies for military applications. This move creates a conflict between Anthropic's role as a security contributor and its institutional standing with the government.
