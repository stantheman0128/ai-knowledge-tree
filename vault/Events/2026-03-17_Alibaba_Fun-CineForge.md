## Alibaba releases Fun-CineForge for cinematic multimodal dubbing

Alibaba's Tongyi Lab introduced Fun-CineForge, a model designed to handle complex dubbing scenarios like facial occlusion and scene changes. It uses a new 'time modality' to ensure precise lip-sync and emotional expression.
