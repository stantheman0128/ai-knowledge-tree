## FIRM addresses reward model hallucinations in RL-optimized image generation

FIRM introduces a robust 8B critic model trained on 600K+ samples to prevent reward model hallucinations during RL-based image generation. By using a Base-and-Bonus strategy, it ensures more reliable feedback for both image editing and text-to-image tasks.
