## Apple approves third-party drivers for eGPU support on Mac

Apple has officially approved drivers developed by Tiny Corp, enabling native support for AMD and Nvidia eGPUs on Apple Silicon devices. This update allows users to run AI large language models on external hardware without needing to disable system security features.
