## OxyGen optimizes VLA multi-task inference through shared KV cache management

OxyGen addresses the inference bottleneck in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models by implementing a unified KV cache management system. By reusing shared visual observations across tasks, it achieves up to 3.7x speedup on models like π₀.₅ without sacrificing performance.
