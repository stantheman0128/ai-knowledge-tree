## Google launches AI initiative to improve heart health in rural Australia

Google has introduced a new AI-driven initiative focused on enhancing cardiovascular health outcomes for residents in remote Australian communities. This project leverages AI technology to bridge the healthcare gap in underserved regions, demonstrating the potential for AI to improve medical accessibility and patient care.
