## Iran's military identifies OpenAI's Stargate data center as a potential strike target

The Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has publicly designated the planned Stargate data center in Abu Dhabi as a retaliatory military target. This development highlights the growing geopolitical risk associated with the concentration of hyperscale AI infrastructure in foreign regions.
