## OpenRouter launches Auto Exacto to improve tool-calling reliability

OpenRouter introduced Auto Exacto, an adaptive routing mechanism that dynamically evaluates model performance every 5 minutes. It aims to reduce tool-calling error rates by selecting the most reliable providers.
