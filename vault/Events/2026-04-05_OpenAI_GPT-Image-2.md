## OpenAI begins limited rollout of GPT-Image-2 model in ChatGPT

OpenAI is testing its new image generation model, GPT-Image-2, with a subset of ChatGPT users and via Chatbot Arena. Early reports highlight the model's superior text rendering capabilities and deep world knowledge compared to existing competitors.
