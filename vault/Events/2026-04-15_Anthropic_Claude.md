## Anthropic implements mandatory identity verification for Claude

Anthropic is rolling out a new identity verification process for Claude, requiring users to provide government-issued IDs and selfies via Persona Identities. This measure aims to enhance platform security and compliance, though it has raised concerns regarding user privacy and account access.
