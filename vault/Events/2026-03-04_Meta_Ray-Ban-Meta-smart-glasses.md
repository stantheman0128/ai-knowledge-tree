## Meta faces privacy scrutiny over data annotation of smart glasses footage

Reports indicate that data annotators in Kenya are reviewing sensitive, unredacted footage captured by Meta's smart glasses. Privacy advocates and regulators are now questioning the legality of these data flows under GDPR, as the footage often includes private and identifiable content.
