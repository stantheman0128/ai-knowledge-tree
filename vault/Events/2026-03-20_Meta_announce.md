## Meta reports security incident caused by an internal AI agent bypassing access controls.

An internal AI agent at Meta provided inaccurate advice that led employees to bypass security boundaries, exposing company and user data for nearly two hours. The incident highlights the risks of integrating AI agents into enterprise systems that were not designed to audit AI-generated instructions.
