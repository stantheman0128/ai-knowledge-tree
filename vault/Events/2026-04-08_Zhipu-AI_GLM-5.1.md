## Zhipu AI releases 754B parameter GLM-5.1 model under MIT license

Zhipu AI has open-sourced its flagship GLM-5.1 model, which features 754 billion parameters and excels in long-range agentic tasks. The model is capable of autonomous operation for up to 8 hours and has set new records on the SWE-Bench Pro benchmark.
