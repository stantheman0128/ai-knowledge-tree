## Tencent AI Lab open-sources LeVo 2 music generation model.

Tencent AI Lab has released the LeVo 2 music model on GitHub, supporting the generation of full songs up to 4.5 minutes long. The model supports multiple languages and claims to match the quality of leading closed-source music models.
