## Self-distillation suppresses epistemic verbalization in LLMs

Research shows that self-distillation causes models to lose their ability to express uncertainty, leading to a 40% drop in out-of-distribution performance. This suggests that current evaluation suites fail to capture the loss of epistemic reasoning.
