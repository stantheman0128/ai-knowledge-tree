## Federal court restricts Perplexity's AI agents from autonomous purchasing

A US District Judge has ordered Perplexity to stop its AI shopping agents from placing orders on Amazon. The court ruled that the agents accessed user accounts without authorization, highlighting legal concerns regarding autonomous AI actions.
