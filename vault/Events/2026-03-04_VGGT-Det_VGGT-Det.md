## Foundation vision models replace sensor calibration for 3D detection.

VGGT-Det leverages internal geometric priors from foundation vision models to perform 3D object detection without explicit camera calibration. This approach significantly improves performance in non-fixed-viewpoint scenarios like drones and mobile devices.
