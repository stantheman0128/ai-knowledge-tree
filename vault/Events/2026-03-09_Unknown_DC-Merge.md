## DC-Merge provides a diagnostic framework for model merging failures

DC-Merge identifies directional consistency in singular space as a key metric for predicting knowledge loss during model merging. By smoothing singular value distributions and aligning geometric structures, it improves performance for both full fine-tuning and LoRA settings.
