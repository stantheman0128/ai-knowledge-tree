## Microsoft restricts Copilot Discord server following 'Microslop' censorship backlash

Microsoft locked down its official Copilot Discord server after attempting to censor the derogatory term 'Microslop' via automated filters. The move backfired, leading to community outcry and increased visibility for the criticism.
