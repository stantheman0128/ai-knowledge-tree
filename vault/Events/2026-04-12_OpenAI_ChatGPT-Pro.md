## OpenAI clarifies ChatGPT Pro quota rules and updates pricing page

OpenAI admitted to confusing language on its pricing page regarding ChatGPT Pro quotas. The company clarified that during the promotional period ending May 31, the $100 and $200 plans offer 10x and 20x the quota of the Plus plan, respectively.
