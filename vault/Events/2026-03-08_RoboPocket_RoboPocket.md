## RoboPocket uses AR to double data collection efficiency for robot imitation learning.

RoboPocket projects a model's predicted trajectory onto a phone screen, allowing human collectors to identify and target weak spots in the policy. This targeted demonstration approach doubles data efficiency compared to traditional offline collection methods.
