## Single-stream architecture enables fast joint audio-video generation.

daVinci-MagiHuman simplifies audio-video generation by using a single sequence of tokens with standard self-attention, removing the need for complex multi-stream architectures. The model achieves high-speed generation and is fully open-sourced.
