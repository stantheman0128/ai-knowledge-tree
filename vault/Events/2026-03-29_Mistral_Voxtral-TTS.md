## Mistral releases Voxtral TTS, becoming the first major LLM lab to offer in-house speech synthesis.

Mistral has launched Voxtral TTS, a speech synthesis model capable of voice cloning from three seconds of audio. This move signals a shift where speech synthesis is becoming a standard capability for LLM platforms rather than a niche vendor service.
