## Andrej Karpathy releases autoresearch for automated LLM training on single GPUs

Andrej Karpathy has open-sourced 'autoresearch', a tool that enables AI agents to autonomously run and iterate on LLM training experiments on a single GPU. The project aims to simulate research community workflows through agentic collaboration.
