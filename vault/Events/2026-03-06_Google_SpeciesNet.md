## Google releases SpeciesNet as an open-source AI model for wildlife conservation

Google has open-sourced SpeciesNet, an AI model designed to assist global efforts in wildlife protection and conservation. By providing this technology to the public, the company aims to empower researchers and conservationists to better monitor and protect biodiversity.
