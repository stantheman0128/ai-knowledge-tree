## MinerU-Diffusion reframes document OCR as parallel inverse rendering.

MinerU-Diffusion replaces traditional autoregressive OCR with a block-wise diffusion decoder. This approach achieves a 3.2x speedup in document parsing by treating the task as inverse rendering.
