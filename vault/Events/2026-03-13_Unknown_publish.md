## Multimodal model performance is heavily influenced by rendering parameters like font choice.

Researchers found that poor visual understanding in multimodal models is often a rendering issue rather than a reasoning deficit. They demonstrated that self-distillation from text-mode reasoning can effectively bridge the modality gap.
