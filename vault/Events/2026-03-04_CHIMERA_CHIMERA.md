## A 4B reasoning model trained on 9K curated samples rivals DeepSeek-R1.

CHIMERA demonstrates that high-quality data curation and domain coverage are more critical than sheer volume for training reasoning models. By using a hierarchical taxonomy to filter 9K samples, a 4B model achieves performance comparable to much larger models like DeepSeek-R1.
