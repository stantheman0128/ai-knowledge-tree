## Anthropic publishes research on AI-driven labor market displacement

Anthropic released a study using Claude usage logs to measure the impact of AI on U.S. employment. The research suggests no systematic increase in unemployment but notes a decline in hiring for younger workers in exposed roles. The study is part of an industry trend of AI companies self-reporting on social impact.
