## Red team competition shows frontier models remain vulnerable to prompt injection.

A 464-person red team competition revealed that all frontier models are susceptible to indirect prompt injection. Claude Opus 4.5 demonstrated the highest resistance, while Gemini 2.5 Pro showed the highest vulnerability.
