## Tempo uses a 6B model to compress long video context for LLMs.

Tempo utilizes a small 6B vision-language model as a scout to select and compress key frames from long videos based on user queries. This method allows large models to outperform competitors like GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 Pro within a strict 8K token budget.
