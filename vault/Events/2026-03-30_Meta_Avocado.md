## Meta delays Avocado model series launch due to performance concerns

Meta is internally testing the 'Avocado' model family, which includes variants for multimodal tasks, tool use, and reasoning. Due to performance trailing behind competitors like GPT-5 and Gemini 3, the company has postponed the official release until at least May 2026.
