## Yann LeCun raises $1 billion to develop AI world models

Yann LeCun has secured $1 billion in funding to build AI systems that learn from physical reality rather than text. The venture aims to create models capable of spatial reasoning and multi-step planning, challenging the current industry reliance on large language models.
