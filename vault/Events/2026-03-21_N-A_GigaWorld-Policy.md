## GigaWorld-Policy decouples video generation for efficient robot deployment

GigaWorld-Policy uses video generation as auxiliary supervision during training but disables it at deployment to improve inference speed. This approach achieved 9x faster inference than Motus with a 7% higher success rate in robotic tasks.
