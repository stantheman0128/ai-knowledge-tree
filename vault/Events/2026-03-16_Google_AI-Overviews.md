## Google's AI Overviews increasingly prioritize internal properties over third-party publishers

Investigations reveal that Google's AI Overviews are routing users primarily to Google-owned platforms like YouTube and Search. This strategy reinforces Google's ecosystem dominance by keeping traffic within its own properties rather than directing it to external websites.
