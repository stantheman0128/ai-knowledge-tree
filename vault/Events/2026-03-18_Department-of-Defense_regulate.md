## Pentagon plans to allow AI companies to train models on classified data in secure facilities.

The U.S. Department of Defense is establishing secure environments for AI firms to train models on classified intelligence. This initiative aims to create military-specific AI variants that remain isolated from civilian oversight and external audits.
