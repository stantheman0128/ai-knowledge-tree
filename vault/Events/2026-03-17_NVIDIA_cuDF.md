## Snap adopts NVIDIA cuDF to accelerate Apache Spark data processing for A/B testing

Snap has integrated NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated cuDF library into its Apache Spark data pipelines on Google Cloud to enhance A/B testing efficiency. This migration has resulted in 4x faster runtimes and 76% cost savings, enabling the company to scale its experimentation infrastructure more sustainably.
