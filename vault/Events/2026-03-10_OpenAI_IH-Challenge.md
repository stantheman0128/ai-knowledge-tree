## OpenAI introduces IH-Challenge to improve instruction hierarchy and safety in frontier LLMs.

OpenAI has released the IH-Challenge, a training framework designed to help models better prioritize trusted instructions. This development enhances safety steerability and strengthens model resistance against prompt injection attacks.
