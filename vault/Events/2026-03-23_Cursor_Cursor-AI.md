## Cursor confirms its coding model is powered by Moonshot AI's Kimi

Cursor admitted that its latest coding assistant features are built on top of the Kimi model from Chinese firm Moonshot AI. The company faced backlash for failing to disclose the model's origin, highlighting growing concerns over supply chain transparency in AI tools.
