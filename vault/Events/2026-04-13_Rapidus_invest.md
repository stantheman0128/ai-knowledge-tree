## Japanese government provides additional 631.5 billion yen to Rapidus for 2nm chip production

The Japanese Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry is increasing its support for Rapidus to accelerate the domestic production of 2nm advanced semiconductors and AI chips. Total government funding for the project has now exceeded 2 trillion yen.
