## Researchers identify semantic fixation as a failure mode in Vision-Language Models

A study of 14 VLMs reveals that models struggle to follow custom rules due to 'semantic fixation' on trained associations. This finding suggests that passing perception benchmarks does not guarantee a model's ability to adhere to specific, non-standard instructions.
