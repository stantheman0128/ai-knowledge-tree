## OpenAI robotics lead Caitlin Kalinowski resigns over Pentagon contract

Caitlin Kalinowski, the head of OpenAI's robotics division, resigned citing moral objections to the company's new agreement with the Department of Defense. Her departure leaves a leadership vacuum in the company's hardware strategy and highlights growing internal friction regarding military partnerships.
