## Qwen reduces free OAuth quota and announces service termination

Qwen has reduced the daily request quota for its free OAuth service from 1,000 to 100 and announced that the free service will be completely discontinued on April 15th. Users are advised to migrate to third-party platforms or Alibaba Cloud's paid services.
