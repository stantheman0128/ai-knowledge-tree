## H Company and NVIDIA release Holotron-12B multimodal model

H Company partnered with NVIDIA to release Holotron-12B, an open-source multimodal model specifically designed for computer-use agents. It utilizes a hybrid SSM-Attention architecture to optimize performance for agentic tasks.
