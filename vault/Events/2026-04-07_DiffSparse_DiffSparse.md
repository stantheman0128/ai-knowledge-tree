## DiffSparse introduces learned token sparsity to cut diffusion inference compute by 54%.

DiffSparse replaces heuristic-based token dropping with a lightweight predictor that learns per-layer, per-step sparsity decisions. This approach achieves a 54% reduction in compute costs for diffusion models like PixArt-α while simultaneously improving generation quality.
