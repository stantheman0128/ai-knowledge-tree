## OpenAI publishes Teen Safety Blueprint for the Japanese market

OpenAI has released a Teen Safety Blueprint in Japan, introducing age verification, parental controls, and well-being safeguards for teenage users. This proactive measure is designed to align with Japan's strict regulatory expectations and build goodwill with local authorities regarding child safety.
