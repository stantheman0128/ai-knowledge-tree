## Helios releases a 14B parameter video model capable of real-time generation on a single GPU.

Helios has introduced a 14B parameter video generation model that achieves 19.5 FPS on a single H100 without relying on common acceleration tricks like KV-cache or quantization. By designing an autoregressive diffusion architecture from scratch, the model reduces compute costs significantly and will be open-sourced to the community.
