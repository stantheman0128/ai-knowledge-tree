## New research challenges the binary consensus between SFT and RL training

Two new papers suggest that supervised finetuning (SFT) can achieve generalization comparable to reinforcement learning under specific conditions, while RL-trained agents may suffer from 'template collapse.' These findings indicate that training method effectiveness is more nuanced than previously assumed, potentially impacting compute allocation strategies.
