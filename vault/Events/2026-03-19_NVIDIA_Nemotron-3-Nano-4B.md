## NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3 Nano 4B for edge AI

NVIDIA has launched the Nemotron 3 Nano 4B, a hybrid architecture model designed for edge-based Agentic AI. It supports a 262K context window and utilizes a combination of Mamba-2 and Transformer layers.
