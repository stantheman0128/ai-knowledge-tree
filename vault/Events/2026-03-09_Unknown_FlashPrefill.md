## FlashPrefill enables near-zero cost sparse attention for long-context inference

FlashPrefill introduces a method to identify attention sparsity patterns at near-zero cost, eliminating the traditional bottleneck of search overhead. The technique delivers a 27.78x speedup on 256K sequences without degrading performance on shorter contexts.
