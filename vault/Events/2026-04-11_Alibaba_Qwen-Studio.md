## Alibaba rebrands Qwen Chat to Qwen Studio

Alibaba has renamed its Qwen Chat platform to Qwen Studio. The platform serves as a unified interface for accessing Qwen's proprietary and open-source models with integrated multimodal and agentic capabilities.
