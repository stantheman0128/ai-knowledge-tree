## Anthropic blacklisted by the U.S. government after failing to reach a Pentagon deal

The U.S. Department of Defense designated Anthropic a national security risk after the company refused to agree to terms that would allow the government to use its models for any lawful purpose. Anthropic's refusal was based on concerns that the Pentagon's requirements would enable mass surveillance of public data.
