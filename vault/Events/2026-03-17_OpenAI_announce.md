## OpenAI Japan launches Japan Teen Safety Blueprint for enhanced youth protection

OpenAI Japan has introduced the Japan Teen Safety Blueprint to implement stricter age verification, parental controls, and well-being safeguards. This initiative aims to create a safer environment for teenage users of generative AI technology.
