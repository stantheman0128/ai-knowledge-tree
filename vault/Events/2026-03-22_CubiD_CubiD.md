## CubiD enables unified 768-D discrete tokens for both visual understanding and generation.

CubiD uses fine-grained masked diffusion to allow high-dimensional discrete tokens to serve both generation and understanding tasks. This approach overcomes the combinatorial explosion typically associated with high-dimensional representations, simplifying the architecture for unified multimodal models.
