## Anthropic forces open-source project OpenCode to remove authentication integrations.

Anthropic issued legal demands to the open-source project OpenCode, forcing the removal of Anthropic-specific authentication plugins and request headers. This move reflects a broader industry shift where AI labs are increasingly enforcing strict boundaries around their proprietary ecosystems.
