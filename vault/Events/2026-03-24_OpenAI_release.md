## OpenAI launches a new Safety Bug Bounty program to identify AI vulnerabilities

OpenAI has introduced a Safety Bug Bounty program aimed at crowdsourcing the identification of security risks such as prompt injection, data exfiltration, and agentic vulnerabilities. This initiative seeks to strengthen the safety and robustness of their AI systems by leveraging the expertise of the global security research community.
