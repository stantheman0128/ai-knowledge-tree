## Gradient Labs leverages OpenAI models to provide AI account managers for banking customers

Gradient Labs is utilizing OpenAI's GPT-4.1 and GPT-5.4 models to power autonomous AI agents for the banking sector. These agents are designed to automate support workflows with high reliability and low latency, enhancing the customer service experience for bank clients.
