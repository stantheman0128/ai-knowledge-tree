## PoCo identifies position encoding as the root cause of identity leakage in multi-shot video generation.

Researchers found that identity swapping in video generation stems from shared positional encoding spaces rather than attention mechanisms. By redesigning position embeddings to carry context control, the team significantly improved cross-shot consistency and reference fidelity.
