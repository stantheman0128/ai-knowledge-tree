## Google integrates Notebooks into Gemini and syncs with NotebookLM

Google has added a Notebooks feature to Gemini, allowing users to create dedicated spaces for managing documents, chat history, and custom instructions. The update enables seamless synchronization with NotebookLM, including access to advanced video overview tools.
