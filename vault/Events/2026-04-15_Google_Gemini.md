## Google updates Gemini app with personalized image generation using Google Photos integration.

Google has introduced new capabilities for the Gemini app that allow users to create personalized images based on their own life context. By leveraging data from Google Photos, the model can generate visuals that reflect the user's unique experiences and personal history.
