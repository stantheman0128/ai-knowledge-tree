## OpenBMB releases VoxCPM2, a 2-billion parameter open-source speech model

OpenBMB has launched VoxCPM2, a 2-billion parameter text-to-speech model based on the MiniCPM-4 architecture. The model supports 30 languages and various Chinese dialects, offering high-quality audio generation and voice cloning capabilities under an Apache-2.0 license.
