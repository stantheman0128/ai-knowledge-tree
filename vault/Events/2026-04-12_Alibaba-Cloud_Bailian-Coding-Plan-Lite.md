## Alibaba Cloud discontinues Bailian Coding Plan Lite

Alibaba Cloud announced it will stop renewals and upgrades for its Bailian Coding Plan Lite starting April 13 due to product strategy adjustments. Existing users can continue using the service until their current subscription expires.
