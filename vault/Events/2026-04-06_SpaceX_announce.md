## SpaceX explores orbital data centers to bypass terrestrial infrastructure constraints

Due to delays in US data center projects caused by power component shortages and tariffs, SpaceX is investigating the feasibility of orbital compute infrastructure. This move reflects the extreme measures companies are considering to meet AI compute demands.
