## New research identifies unique failure modes in Diffusion Large Language Models

A new ACL paper reveals that Diffusion LLMs hallucinate differently than autoregressive models, exhibiting premature termination, incomplete denoising, and context intrusion. These findings suggest that existing hallucination detection tools are ineffective for dLLMs and require new, purpose-built reliability testing.
