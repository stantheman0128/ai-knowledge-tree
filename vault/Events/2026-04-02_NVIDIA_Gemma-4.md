## NVIDIA collaborates with Google to optimize Gemma 4 models for local AI execution on RTX and edge devices.

NVIDIA has partnered with Google to optimize the new Gemma 4 open model family for high-performance execution on NVIDIA GPUs, ranging from Jetson edge modules to RTX-powered workstations. This collaboration enables developers to run efficient, low-latency agentic AI locally, leveraging NVIDIA's Tensor Cores and CUDA software stack for superior performance.
