## OpenAI pledges $1 billion to charity and launches shopping features in ChatGPT

OpenAI announced a $1 billion philanthropic commitment to various social causes while simultaneously introducing a new shopping experience within ChatGPT. The new commerce features, powered by an 'Agentic Commerce Protocol,' allow users to browse and purchase products directly through the chatbot interface.
