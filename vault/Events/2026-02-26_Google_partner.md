## Google partners with Massachusetts AI Hub to provide free AI training

Google has launched a new initiative in collaboration with the Massachusetts AI Hub to offer free AI training resources to all residents of the Commonwealth. This partnership aims to increase AI literacy and accessibility across the state by leveraging Google's educational content.
