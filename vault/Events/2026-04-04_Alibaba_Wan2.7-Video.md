## Alibaba Tongyi Lab releases Wan2.7-Video generative model

Alibaba's Tongyi Lab has launched Wan2.7-Video, a new video generation model supporting full-modal inputs. It features advanced capabilities like precise local editing, character locking, and complex camera movement control.
