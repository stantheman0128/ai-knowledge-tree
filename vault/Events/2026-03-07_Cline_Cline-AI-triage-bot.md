## Security researcher discloses 'Clinejection' vulnerability in AI-powered triage bot

A security researcher demonstrated that a prompt injection attack on an AI triage bot could escalate to full production pipeline access. The vulnerability stemmed from excessive permissions granted to the AI agent without proper sandboxing or human review. This incident highlights critical risks in integrating AI agents into CI/CD workflows.
