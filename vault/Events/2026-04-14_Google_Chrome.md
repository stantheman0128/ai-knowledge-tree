## Google introduces Skills in Chrome to turn AI prompts into one-click tools

Google has launched a new feature called Skills in Chrome that allows users to save, discover, and remix AI workflows. This update enables users to repeat complex AI tasks instantly, streamlining productivity by turning frequent prompts into accessible one-click tools.
