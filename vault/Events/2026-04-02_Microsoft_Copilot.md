## Microsoft classifies Copilot as 'entertainment only' while integrating it into professional workflows

Microsoft's terms of service label Copilot as an entertainment product, creating a legal contradiction with its deployment in professional code review environments. This discrepancy highlights the tension between product teams pushing for deep integration and legal teams attempting to mitigate liability.
