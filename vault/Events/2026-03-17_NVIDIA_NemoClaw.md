## NVIDIA unveils NemoClaw and new open models to power local AI agents on RTX PCs and DGX Spark

NVIDIA introduced NemoClaw, an open-source stack designed to optimize OpenClaw agent performance on local hardware, enhancing privacy and reducing token costs. The company also released new open models, including Nemotron 3 Super and Nano, to enable high-performance AI agent execution on RTX PCs and DGX Spark workstations.
