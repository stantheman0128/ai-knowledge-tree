## OpenAI publishes a framework for defending AI agents against prompt injection.

OpenAI released research on mitigating prompt injection, arguing that the problem cannot be fully solved and requires robust system design. The company recommends that agents should only perform limited, reversible actions and require human confirmation for consequential tasks.
