## NVIDIA launches Nemotron Coalition to advance open frontier AI models

NVIDIA has formed the Nemotron Coalition, a global collaboration of AI labs and model builders dedicated to advancing open-source frontier foundation models. The initiative will begin with a base model co-developed by Mistral AI and NVIDIA, aiming to foster an ecosystem where open and proprietary models coexist to solve complex industry challenges.
