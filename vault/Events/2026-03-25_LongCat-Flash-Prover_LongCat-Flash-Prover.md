## Formal proof generation improved by decomposing tasks into autoformalization, scaffolding, and proving.

Researchers introduced a methodology that splits formal proof generation into three independent subtasks, each optimized via reinforcement learning. This approach outperforms end-to-end training by addressing specific bottlenecks in the reasoning chain, and includes HisPO to stabilize MoE training.
