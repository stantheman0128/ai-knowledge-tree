## Research shows RLVR provides inherent robustness against noisy training labels.

This study demonstrates that reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) naturally filters out noisy labels because the model must independently reproduce an answer to receive a training signal. This structural advantage allows for high performance even with noise ratios as high as 0.9.
