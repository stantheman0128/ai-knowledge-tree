## OmniJigsaw improves cross-modal reasoning through temporal reordering.

OmniJigsaw uses a self-supervised proxy task where models must reassemble shuffled audio and video segments. By masking modalities to prevent shortcuts, the model learns deeper integration between hearing and seeing without human annotations.
