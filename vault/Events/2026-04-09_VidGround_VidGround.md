## Research reveals video understanding models rely heavily on text bias.

Two independent papers show that 40-60% of video benchmark questions can be answered using text cues alone without watching the video. The findings suggest that current video understanding progress is partially illusory and emphasizes the need for higher-quality, visually-grounded training data.
