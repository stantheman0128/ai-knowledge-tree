## Utonia provides a unified encoder for diverse point cloud domains.

Utonia is a self-supervised Transformer encoder capable of processing five distinct point cloud domains, from LiDAR to CAD models. By unifying these representations, it enables emergent behaviors and improves downstream performance in robotics and spatial reasoning.
