## Nvidia invests $4 billion into photonics companies Lumentum and Coherent

Nvidia is investing $2 billion each into Lumentum and Coherent to advance optical data center technology. The move aims to address bottlenecks in data transmission speeds between GPUs, which are becoming a critical constraint in AI infrastructure.
