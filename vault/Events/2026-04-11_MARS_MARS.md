## MARS enables multi-token generation via fine-tuning without architectural changes.

MARS introduces a method to achieve multi-token prediction through standard instruction-tuning rather than complex architectural modifications. This approach increases throughput by 1.5-1.7x while maintaining full compatibility with existing single-token models.
