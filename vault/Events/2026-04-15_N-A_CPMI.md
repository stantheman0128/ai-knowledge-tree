## Contrastive Pointwise Mutual Information reduces process reward labeling costs

Researchers introduced CPMI, a method that uses model-internal probabilities to evaluate reasoning steps without requiring expensive Monte Carlo rollouts. This approach achieves an 84% reduction in dataset construction time and is accepted at ACL.
