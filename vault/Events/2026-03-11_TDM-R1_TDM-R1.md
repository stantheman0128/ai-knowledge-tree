## TDM-R1 enables non-differentiable reward training for few-step diffusion models.

Researchers introduced TDM-R1, a method that allows few-step diffusion models to be trained using non-differentiable rewards like human preference and safety flags. By using a proxy model to extract per-step feedback, the approach achieves superior performance over 100-step baselines in just 4 steps.
