## Analysis reveals Google's AI Overviews maintain a 9% error rate despite model upgrades.

Independent testing by Oumi indicates that Google's AI Overviews provide incorrect information in 9% of searches, a rate that persists even after upgrading from Gemini 2 to Gemini 3. While the model has improved in factual accuracy, it has become less transparent, with a higher percentage of answers lacking verifiable sources. Google disputes the methodology, arguing that the benchmark used does not accurately reflect real-world search patterns.
