## NVIDIA donates its Dynamic Resource Allocation Driver for GPUs to the Kubernetes community

NVIDIA has donated its Dynamic Resource Allocation (DRA) Driver for GPUs to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation to foster community-driven innovation in AI infrastructure. This move transitions the driver to full community ownership, enabling better GPU orchestration, scalability, and efficiency for enterprises running AI workloads on Kubernetes.
