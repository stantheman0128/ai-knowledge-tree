## OpenAI rotates macOS signing certificates following axios supply chain attack

OpenAI rotated its macOS code signing certificates after a compromised version of the axios library affected its toolchain. While no user data was compromised, the incident highlights the risks of transitive dependencies in AI products marketed for sensitive sectors like healthcare.
