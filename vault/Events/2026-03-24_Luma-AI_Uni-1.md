## Luma AI releases Uni-1 multimodal model for reasoning and vision

Luma AI introduced Uni-1, a multimodal model that integrates logical reasoning with visual generation in a single architecture. It achieves state-of-the-art performance on the RISEBench benchmark and excels in human preference evaluations for style and editing.
