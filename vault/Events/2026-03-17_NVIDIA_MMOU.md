## NVIDIA releases MMOU benchmark for long-video multimodal understanding

NVIDIA has introduced the MMOU benchmark to evaluate joint visual, audio, and text reasoning in long-form videos. The benchmark aims to expose current limitations in cross-modal reasoning capabilities.
