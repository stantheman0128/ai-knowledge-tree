## New research identifies failure modes in on-policy distillation for long sequences.

A study reveals that sampled-token on-policy distillation is fundamentally fragile on long sequences due to single-token signal degradation. The authors provide a diagnostic checklist and fixes, including truncated reverse-KL and special-token masking, to improve stability.
