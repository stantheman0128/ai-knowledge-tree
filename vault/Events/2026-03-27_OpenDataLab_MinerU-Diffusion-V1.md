## OpenDataLab releases MinerU-Diffusion-V1 for document OCR

OpenDataLab has introduced MinerU-Diffusion-V1, a document OCR framework that uses block-level parallel diffusion decoding. This approach significantly reduces sequence latency compared to traditional autoregressive methods.
