## Xiaohongshu releases FireRed-OCR model

Xiaohongshu's REDtech team has open-sourced FireRed-OCR, a model based on Qwen3-VL-2B. It is designed to handle complex document processing tasks like formula parsing and table reconstruction with high accuracy.
