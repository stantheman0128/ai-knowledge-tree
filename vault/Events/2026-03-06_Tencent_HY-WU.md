## Tencent Hunyuan team open-sources HY-WU framework for image editing.

Tencent's Hunyuan team has released the HY-WU framework, which enables high-fidelity text-guided image editing. It supports complex tasks like virtual try-on and face swapping without requiring test-time optimization.
