## Fish Audio launches STT tool with multilingual and emotion tagging

Fish Audio has introduced a new speech-to-text tool that supports over 100 languages and automatically detects emotional cues like laughter or sighing. The output is compatible with their S2 TTS model for seamless voice production workflows.
