## ACE Studio and StepFun release ACE-Step 1.5 XL music model

ACE Studio and StepFun have jointly released the ACE-Step 1.5 XL music generation model. The 4B parameter model is open-sourced under the MIT license and reportedly outperforms Suno v5 in internal evaluations.
