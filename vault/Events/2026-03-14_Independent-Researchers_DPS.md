## Dynamics-Predictive Sampling (DPS) optimizes RL fine-tuning for reasoning models.

DPS addresses the limitation of only training on 'just hard enough' problems by using dynamical systems to predict problem-solving progress. This method improves long-term capability ceilings in reasoning models and was accepted at ICLR.
