## Cursor admits Composer 2 is based on Kimi K2.5

Cursor acknowledged that their new Composer 2 model uses Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.5 as a base, following developer scrutiny. Moonshot AI confirmed a formal licensing partnership between the two companies facilitated by Fireworks AI.
