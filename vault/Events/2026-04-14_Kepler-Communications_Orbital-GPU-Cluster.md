## Kepler Communications begins commercial orders for its orbital GPU cluster

Kepler Communications is now accepting orders for its space-based compute cluster, which provides edge inference for satellite applications. By placing GPUs in low Earth orbit, the company offers low-latency compute solutions where terrestrial data centers are not viable.
