## OpenAI to introduce ads to free and low-cost ChatGPT users in the US

OpenAI is expanding its revenue model by introducing advertisements to free and 'Go' tier ChatGPT users in the United States. This move is intended to help the company offset the rising costs of AI infrastructure and remain competitive.
