## NVIDIA argues that cost per token is the critical metric for AI infrastructure economics

NVIDIA advocates for a shift in how enterprises evaluate AI infrastructure, moving away from traditional metrics like FLOPS per dollar toward 'cost per token.' The company demonstrates that its Blackwell architecture significantly outperforms previous generations by delivering vastly higher token throughput, ultimately lowering the total cost of ownership for AI inference.
