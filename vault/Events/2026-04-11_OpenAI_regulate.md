## OpenAI supports Illinois bill to limit AI model provider liability

OpenAI has endorsed an Illinois bill that would exempt AI companies from lawsuits regarding harms caused by their model outputs. This move aims to shift legal responsibility toward individual users who apply AI tools for harmful purposes, such as generating deepfakes.
