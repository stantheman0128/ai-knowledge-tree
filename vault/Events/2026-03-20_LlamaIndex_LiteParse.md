## LlamaIndex open-sources LiteParse for local document parsing

LlamaIndex has open-sourced LiteParse, a JavaScript-based tool for local document parsing designed for AI agents. It supports over 50 file formats and can process 500-page documents in approximately two seconds without requiring a GPU.
