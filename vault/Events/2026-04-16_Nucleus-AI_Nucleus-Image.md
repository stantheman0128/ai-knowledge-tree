## Nucleus AI open-sources 17B parameter Nucleus-Image model

Nucleus AI released Nucleus-Image, an open-source text-to-image model using a Mixture-of-Experts architecture. With 17B total parameters and 2B active parameters, it reportedly matches or exceeds the performance of GPT Image 1 and Imagen 4.
