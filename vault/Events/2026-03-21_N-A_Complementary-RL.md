## Complementary RL addresses experience replay degradation in agent training

Researchers introduced Complementary RL to solve the stale experience replay problem in agent training. By co-optimizing the experience extractor and policy network, the system ensures experience quality evolves alongside policy performance, leading to a 10% improvement in single-task performance.
