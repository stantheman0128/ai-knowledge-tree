## OpenAI Foundation announces $1 billion commitment to social impact initiatives

The OpenAI Foundation has pledged to invest at least $1 billion toward projects focused on curing diseases, fostering economic opportunity, and enhancing AI resilience. This initiative marks a significant expansion of the organization's philanthropic efforts to ensure the benefits of AI are broadly distributed.
