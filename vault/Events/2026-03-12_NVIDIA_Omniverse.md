## NVIDIA and Dassault Systèmes partner to advance industrial AI and digital twin technology

NVIDIA and Dassault Systèmes have announced a strategic partnership to integrate NVIDIA's accelerated computing and AI physics models with Dassault Systèmes' virtual twin platforms. This collaboration aims to accelerate industrial design, simulation, and manufacturing processes across various sectors, including automotive, life sciences, and aerospace.
