## S2D2 introduces a training-free acceleration method for diffusion language models.

S2D2 enables faster decoding for diffusion language models by using the same model as both a parallel drafter and a sequential verifier. This approach achieves up to 4.7x acceleration without requiring auxiliary models or complex training.
