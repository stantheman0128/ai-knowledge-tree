## OpenAI introduces new resources and certifications to support AI integration in education.

OpenAI has launched a suite of tools, certifications, and measurement resources designed to help educational institutions bridge AI capability gaps. This initiative aims to expand learning opportunities and ensure that AI is effectively and equitably integrated into school and university curricula.
