## Google Research releases Vibe Coding XR for WebXR development

Google Research introduced Vibe Coding XR, a tool that uses natural language and Gemini to generate interactive, physics-aware WebXR applications in under 60 seconds. The project is open-source and aimed at rapid prototyping.
