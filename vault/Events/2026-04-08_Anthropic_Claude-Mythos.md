## Anthropic restricts access to its powerful Claude Mythos model due to security risks.

Anthropic has introduced Project Glasswing, a framework that limits access to its highly capable Claude Mythos model to a select group of twelve partners and critical infrastructure organizations. The decision stems from the model's autonomous ability to identify and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities, which Anthropic fears could be weaponized if released broadly. The company is also providing $100 million in usage credits and funding for open-source security organizations.
