## Study reveals structural trade-off between temporal and spatial understanding in Video-SFT

A systematic study demonstrates that fine-tuning multimodal LLMs on video data consistently degrades static image understanding. This structural trade-off persists across architectures and scales, suggesting that temporal gains come at a significant spatial cost.
