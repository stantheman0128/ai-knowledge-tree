## ByteDance pauses global release of Seedance 2.0 due to copyright dispute with Disney

ByteDance has halted the global launch of its video generation model Seedance 2.0 following a cease-and-desist from Disney. The company is currently addressing legal risks and improving content safety mechanisms.
