## OptiMer optimizes data mixing ratios for continual pre-training

Researchers introduced OptiMer, a method that treats data mixing ratios as a post-training optimization target rather than a pre-training hyperparameter. By using Bayesian optimization on distribution vectors, the technique reduces search costs by 15-35x compared to traditional data mixing.
