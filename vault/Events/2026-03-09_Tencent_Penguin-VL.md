## Penguin-VL uses text-only LLM encoders to outperform CLIP-based vision models

Tencent researchers found that contrastive pretraining in CLIP models suppresses fine-grained visual detail. By initializing vision encoders from text-only LLMs, Penguin-VL achieves superior performance in document understanding and visual reasoning at the 2B and 8B scale.
