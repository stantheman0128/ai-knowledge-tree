## Federal court upholds government blacklisting of Anthropic

A federal appeals court denied Anthropic's motion to stay a government blacklisting order, effectively barring the company from federal procurement contracts. This decision highlights a disconnect between corporate safety investments and national security reviews, leaving the company excluded from government markets.
