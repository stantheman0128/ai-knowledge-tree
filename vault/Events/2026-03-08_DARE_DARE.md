## DARE improves RAG retrieval by encoding data distribution features into vectors.

DARE addresses the failure of RAG systems to account for statistical preconditions by embedding data distribution features into retrieval vectors. This method significantly outperforms existing open-source embedding models in retrieving appropriate R functions for statistical tasks.
