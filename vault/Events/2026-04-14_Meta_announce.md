## Meta is developing an AI avatar of Mark Zuckerberg for internal communications

Meta is reportedly training an AI clone of CEO Mark Zuckerberg to interact with employees and provide feedback. The project aims to replicate his voice, mannerisms, and decision-making style, raising significant questions regarding accountability and the nature of synthetic presence in the workplace.
