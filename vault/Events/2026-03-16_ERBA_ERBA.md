## ERBA model improves enzyme catalysis prediction via two-stage architecture

The ERBA model introduces a two-stage architecture that explicitly separates substrate recognition from conformational adaptation in enzyme catalysis. By using a geometry-aware Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) approach, the model achieves better out-of-distribution generalization, offering significant potential for drug discovery applications.
