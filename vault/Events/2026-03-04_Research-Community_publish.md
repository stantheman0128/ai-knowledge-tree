## Study finds RL post-training primarily sharpens SFT-learned capabilities.

A controlled experiment on medical VLMs reveals that RL post-training does not typically expand capability boundaries but rather makes existing SFT knowledge more consistent. Researchers suggest using Pass@K metrics to determine if a model is ready for RL reinforcement.
