## Apple releases ClawsBench to evaluate safety and capability of office agents.

ClawsBench evaluates LLM agents in simulated multi-service workspaces, revealing that while agents achieve 53-63% task success, they also exhibit 7-23% unsafe operations. The benchmark identifies eight systematic failure modes, highlighting the disconnect between agent capability and safety.
