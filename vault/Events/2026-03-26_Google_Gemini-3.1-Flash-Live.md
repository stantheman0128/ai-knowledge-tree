## Google releases Gemini 3.1 Flash Live with support for 90 languages

Google has launched Gemini 3.1 Flash Live, a high-performance audio and voice model designed for real-time interaction. It features improved latency, noise resistance, and instruction following, and is now available to developers via API and to consumers through Gemini Live and Search Live.
