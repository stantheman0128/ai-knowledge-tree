## Spatial-TTT uses test-time training to improve long-video spatial understanding

Spatial-TTT updates model parameters at test time to learn spatial structures dynamically, avoiding the need for massive context windows. This approach achieves state-of-the-art results on long-video tasks by focusing on active geometric correspondence rather than passive frame memorization.
