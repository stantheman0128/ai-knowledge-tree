## Suno faces licensing impasse with major music labels as AI-generated music dominates charts.

Suno is currently deadlocked in licensing negotiations with Universal Music Group and Sony Music Entertainment regarding the distribution of AI-generated music. While major labels seek to restrict AI tracks to within specific apps, Suno advocates for open distribution on streaming platforms. This conflict highlights a growing regulatory vacuum as AI-generated artists like Eddie Dalton begin to outperform human musicians on commercial charts.
