## New benchmark reveals limited effectiveness of agent skill injection.

SWE-Skills-Bench provides a rigorous evaluation of 49 public software engineering skills, finding that most provide negligible performance gains. The study highlights that generic skill injection is often ineffective and can even degrade performance due to context conflicts.
