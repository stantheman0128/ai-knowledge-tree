## Ai2 releases WildDet3D open-source monocular 3D detection model

The Allen Institute for AI has open-sourced WildDet3D, a model capable of predicting 3D spatial information from single RGB images. It supports various prompt types and includes an iOS demo for real-time AR rendering.
