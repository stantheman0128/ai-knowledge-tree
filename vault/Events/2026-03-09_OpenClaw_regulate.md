## Shenzhen Longgang district proposes support for OpenClaw amid MIIT security warnings

The Longgang district in Shenzhen has drafted policies to support the development of OpenClaw and OPC. Simultaneously, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) issued a security warning regarding potential vulnerabilities in OpenClaw's default configurations.
