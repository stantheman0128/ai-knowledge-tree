## Developer claims to have reverse-engineered Google's SynthID watermarking system

A developer published code claiming to strip and inject SynthID watermarks, potentially undermining Google's AI content verification tool. Google has denied the validity of the claims, while the incident highlights the ongoing battle between AI safety tools and adversarial probing.
