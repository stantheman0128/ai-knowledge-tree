## Elon Musk announces plans for a custom chip manufacturing plant in Texas

Tesla and SpaceX are partnering to build a 'Terafab' chip plant in Austin to produce custom silicon for humanoid robots and xAI training. The move aims to address the ongoing global shortage of AI-capable hardware.
