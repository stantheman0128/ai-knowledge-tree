## ElevenLabs upgrades Scribe v2 with entity redaction and expanded keyword prompts

ElevenLabs has updated its Scribe v2 speech-to-text model with new features including automatic sensitive information redaction and a 'No Verbatim' mode. The capacity for keyword prompting has also been increased to 1,000 terms.
