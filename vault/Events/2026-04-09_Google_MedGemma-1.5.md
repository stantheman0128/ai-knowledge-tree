## Google releases MedGemma 1.5 for multimodal medical imaging analysis.

MedGemma 1.5 is a 4B parameter model capable of processing CT/MRI volumes, pathology slides, and longitudinal chest X-rays within a single architecture. This represents a unified approach to complex medical imaging tasks.
