## Cohere releases open-source speech recognition model Cohere Transcribe

Cohere has open-sourced its 2-billion parameter speech recognition model, Cohere Transcribe, under the Apache 2.0 license. The model supports 14 languages and is optimized for high-throughput, efficient audio-to-text transcription.
