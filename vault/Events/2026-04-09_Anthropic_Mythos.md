## Anthropic launches Project Glasswing to restrict access to cybersecurity model Mythos

Anthropic introduced Project Glasswing, a framework that limits access to its cybersecurity-focused model, Mythos, to vetted researchers. The company aims to prevent misuse while allowing for the patching of critical software vulnerabilities.
