## Google launches Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite with configurable thinking tiers

Google introduced Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, a highly cost-effective model designed for high-volume production workloads. It features four configurable thinking levels, allowing developers to balance reasoning depth against inference speed and cost.
