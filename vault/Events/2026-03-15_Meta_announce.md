## Meta considers 20% workforce reduction to fund AI infrastructure

Meta is reportedly planning to lay off approximately 14,000 employees to reallocate capital toward AI infrastructure, data centers, and talent. This restructuring highlights the company's aggressive pivot to prioritize AI development over existing business lines.
