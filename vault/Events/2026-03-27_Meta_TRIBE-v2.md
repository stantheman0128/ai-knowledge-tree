## Meta releases TRIBE v2 to predict human brain fMRI responses

Meta has unveiled TRIBE v2, a multimodal foundation model designed for neuroscience research. It can predict human fMRI brain responses based on video, audio, and text inputs, aiming to unify cognitive neuroscience modeling.
