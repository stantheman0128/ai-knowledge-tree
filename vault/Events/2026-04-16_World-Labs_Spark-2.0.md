## World Labs open-sources Spark 2.0 for web-based 3D rendering

World Labs has open-sourced Spark 2.0, a dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting renderer optimized for web browsers. It enables the streaming and rendering of massive 3D scenes with over 100 million Gaussians on devices ranging from mobile to VR.
