## Fine-tuned LLMs outperform GPT-5 in supply chain disruption forecasting.

A study demonstrated that fine-tuning LLMs on real-world disruption events significantly outperforms GPT-5's zero-shot capabilities in accuracy and calibration. The model spontaneously developed structured probabilistic reasoning, suggesting that domain-specific fine-tuning is superior for predicting low-frequency, high-impact events. This methodology could be applied to financial risk and geopolitical forecasting.
