## New research identifies visual representation degradation in multimodal models.

A CVPR paper reveals that training multimodal LLMs on language data systematically erodes visual fidelity due to the text generation objective. The authors propose Predictive Regularization (PRe) to constrain mid-layer features and preserve visual information.
