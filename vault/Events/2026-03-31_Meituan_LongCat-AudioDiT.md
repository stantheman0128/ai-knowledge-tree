## Meituan open-sources LongCat-AudioDiT text-to-speech model

Meituan's LongCat team released the LongCat-AudioDiT model, a non-autoregressive diffusion-based TTS system. It supports high-fidelity Chinese and English speech generation with zero-shot cloning capabilities.
