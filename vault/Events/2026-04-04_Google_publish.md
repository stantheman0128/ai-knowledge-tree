## Google researchers identify single MLP neurons that encode specific entities in LLMs.

Researchers discovered that individual MLP neurons in early layers of LLMs can act as switches for specific entities, allowing for precise knowledge editing. By ablating or injecting activations into these neurons, they could trigger or reverse entity-level knowledge recall. This finding suggests that knowledge storage in models is more sparse and locatable than previously assumed.
