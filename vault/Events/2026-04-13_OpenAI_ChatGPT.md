## OpenAI launches department-specific ChatGPT adoption playbooks for business functions

OpenAI simultaneously released ChatGPT training guides for marketing, operations, finance, and sales to accelerate enterprise adoption. By targeting business lines directly rather than IT departments, OpenAI aims to create interlocking workflows that increase switching costs and deepen product integration.
