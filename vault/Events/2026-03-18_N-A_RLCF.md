## Research on using community citation signals to train AI 'taste'

Researchers introduced Reinforcement Learning from Community Feedback (RLCF), which uses citation data to train models to evaluate research potential. The resulting 'Scientific Judge' outperforms GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro in predicting paper impact, demonstrating that taste-based decision-making can be a learnable capability.
