## Anthropic introduces Harness design for long-running AI applications

Anthropic has published a technical guide on the 'Harness' design pattern, which uses a multi-agent architecture to improve Claude's performance in long-running, low-intervention tasks. The approach utilizes separate planner, generator, and evaluator agents to ensure quality and stability.
