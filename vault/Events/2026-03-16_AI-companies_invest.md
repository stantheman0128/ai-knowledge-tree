## AI companies hire improv actors to train models on human emotional expression

AI firms are recruiting improv actors to generate training data that captures nuanced human emotions for foundation models. While intended to improve conversational realism, this trend coincides with growing legal concerns regarding the potential for emotionally manipulative AI to cause harm.
