## Nvidia announces DLSS 5, a generative AI-based graphics rendering technology

Nvidia introduced DLSS 5, which uses generative AI to create visual details and textures that were not present in the original game render. While Nvidia claims it boosts photorealism, the technology has sparked controversy among gamers and developers over the loss of artistic control.
