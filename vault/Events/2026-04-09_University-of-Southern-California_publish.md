## USC researchers warn of AI-driven cognitive homogenization

A research paper published in Trends in Cognitive Sciences suggests that LLMs cause users to converge on similar reasoning styles, potentially reducing cognitive diversity. The authors argue that reliance on AI models may erode original thought and abstract reasoning.
