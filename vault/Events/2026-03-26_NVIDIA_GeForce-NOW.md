## NVIDIA adds five new game titles to the GeForce NOW cloud gaming library

NVIDIA has expanded its GeForce NOW cloud gaming service by adding five new titles, including Screamer and the latest update for Honkai: Star Rail. This update reinforces the platform's commitment to providing instant access to popular games across various devices without the need for local installations.
