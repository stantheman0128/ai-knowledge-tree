## OpenAI releases framework to measure AI impact on student learning

OpenAI launched a suite of tools developed with academic partners to track how ChatGPT influences student learning outcomes. Early findings suggest that students using AI tools show improved exam performance. The framework is intended to address regulatory and public concerns regarding AI's role in education.
