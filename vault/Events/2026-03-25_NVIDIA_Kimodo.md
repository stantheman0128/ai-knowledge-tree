## NVIDIA open-sources Kimodo motion generation tool for humanoid robots

NVIDIA has released Kimodo, a motion diffusion model trained on 700 hours of commercial motion capture data. It enables the generation of high-quality 3D movements for humanoid robots like the Unitree G1 based on text prompts or path constraints.
