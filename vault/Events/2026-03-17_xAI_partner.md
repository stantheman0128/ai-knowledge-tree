## Senator Elizabeth Warren challenges Pentagon's decision to grant xAI access to classified networks

Senator Elizabeth Warren has formally questioned the Pentagon regarding its decision to grant xAI access to classified military networks. She argues that the company's ongoing safety issues with its consumer chatbot, Grok, should disqualify it from handling sensitive national security information.
