## TorchUMM codebase unifies multimodal model evaluation and training

TorchUMM provides a unified codebase for mainstream multimodal models, enabling standardized apples-to-apples comparisons across understanding, generation, and editing tasks. It aims to solve the fragmentation issue in multimodal research by providing consistent evaluation protocols.
