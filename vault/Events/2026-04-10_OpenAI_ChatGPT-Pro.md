## OpenAI launches $100/month ChatGPT Pro subscription tier

OpenAI has introduced a premium subscription tier priced at $100 per month, five times the cost of its Plus plan. The move reflects the industry's struggle to balance high inference costs with consumer pricing, as the company attempts to close margin gaps by targeting power users.
