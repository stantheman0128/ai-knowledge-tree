## Sarvam AI open-sources Sarvam-30B and Sarvam-105B models

Sarvam AI has released two MoE-based models, Sarvam-30B and Sarvam-105B, which are optimized for Indian languages. The models are available on Hugging Face and via API, offering high performance for local and complex reasoning tasks.
