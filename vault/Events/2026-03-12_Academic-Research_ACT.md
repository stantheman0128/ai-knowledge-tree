## Agentic Critical Training (ACT) enables models to learn judgment through RL.

ACT trains agents to evaluate the quality of candidate actions using reinforcement learning rather than just imitating expert demonstrations. This method improves generalization to out-of-distribution tasks by teaching the model to develop its own critical judgment.
