## DISCO uses diffusion models to design enzymes without human-specified blueprints.

DISCO generates protein sequences and 3D structures from scratch based on reaction intermediates, bypassing the need for human-defined catalytic sites. The AI-designed enzymes outperform human-engineered ones and demonstrate the potential for exploring new chemical spaces.
