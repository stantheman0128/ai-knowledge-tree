## Atlassian announces 1,600 job cuts to pivot toward AI product development.

Atlassian is laying off 1,600 employees to shift resources toward AI, despite the risk that AI-driven productivity gains may reduce the overall demand for their collaboration software. The company faces a strategic challenge as its core products are designed for larger teams that AI tools are currently helping to shrink.
