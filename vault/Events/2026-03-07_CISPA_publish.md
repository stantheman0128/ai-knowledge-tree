## Research exposes widespread fraud in Shadow API model services

Researchers at CISPA published a study revealing that nearly half of third-party 'Shadow API' services engage in fraudulent activities, such as misrepresenting models. This practice leads to significant performance degradation and poses risks to scientific reproducibility.
