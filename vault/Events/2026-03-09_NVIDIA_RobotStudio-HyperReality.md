## ABB Robotics integrates NVIDIA Omniverse to scale industrial physical AI

ABB Robotics is partnering with NVIDIA to integrate Omniverse libraries into its RobotStudio suite, creating a new product called RobotStudio HyperReality. This integration enables high-fidelity, physically accurate simulation to bridge the 'sim-to-real' gap, allowing manufacturers to train and validate AI-powered robots virtually before physical deployment.
