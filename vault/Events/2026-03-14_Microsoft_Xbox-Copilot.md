## Microsoft to bring Gaming Copilot to Xbox consoles

Microsoft announced that its Gaming Copilot AI assistant will arrive on current-generation Xbox consoles later this year. The tool is designed to embed within the gaming ecosystem to assist players, representing Microsoft's push to make AI the primary interface for its gaming platform.
