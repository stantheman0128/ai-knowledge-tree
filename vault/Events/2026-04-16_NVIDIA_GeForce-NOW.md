## Capcom’s PRAGMATA joins GeForce NOW library alongside service expansion to India

NVIDIA has added Capcom’s new sci-fi title PRAGMATA to its GeForce NOW cloud gaming platform on launch day, featuring support for DLSS 4. Additionally, the company announced the expansion of its GeForce NOW Ultimate membership to the Indian market via a new beta service.
