## NVIDIA and ComfyUI streamline local AI video generation for creators at GDC

NVIDIA announced a suite of updates at GDC to optimize AI video generation on RTX PCs, including a simplified App View for ComfyUI and native support for high-performance NVFP4 and FP8 model formats. Additionally, the company introduced RTX Video Super Resolution for ComfyUI and a new Python package to enable 30x faster 4K video upscaling for developers.
