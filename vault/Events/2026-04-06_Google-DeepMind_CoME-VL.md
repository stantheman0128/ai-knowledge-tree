## CoME-VL fuses CLIP and DINO encoders to improve spatial grounding in vision-language models.

CoME-VL addresses CLIP's structural spatial blindness by fusing it with DINO representations. This method improves grounding and detection tasks, offering a modular solution for developers to enhance existing VLM pipelines.
