## OpenAI pivots strategy toward IPO readiness by deprioritizing consumer side projects.

OpenAI is restructuring its operations to focus on enterprise revenue and IPO preparation, shelving several consumer-facing projects like Sora and Atlas. The company is also pursuing large-scale joint ventures to accelerate its enterprise product adoption.
