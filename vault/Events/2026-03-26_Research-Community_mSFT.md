## mSFT optimizes multi-task fine-tuning by dropping overfitted sub-datasets.

mSFT addresses compute waste in multi-task fine-tuning by iteratively identifying and dropping sub-datasets that overfit early. This method reduces FLOPs while improving performance, particularly under constrained compute budgets.
