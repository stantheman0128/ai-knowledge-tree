## Mistral AI releases Mistral Small 4 open-source MoE model

Mistral AI has launched Mistral Small 4, a 119B parameter Mixture-of-Experts model under the Apache 2.0 license. It features a 256k context window and native multimodal capabilities with improved latency and throughput.
