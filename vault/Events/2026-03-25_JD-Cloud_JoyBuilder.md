## JD Cloud launches AI programming subscription service on JoyBuilder

JD Cloud has introduced a new 'Coding Plan' subscription service for its JoyBuilder platform, offering access to models like Kimi-K2.5 and DeepSeek-V3.2. The service is targeted at individual developers with competitive pricing for code assistance tools.
