## Pearl Abyss apologizes for using AI-generated art in Crimson Desert

Pearl Abyss issued a public apology after players discovered AI-generated art assets in their game, Crimson Desert. The studio claimed the assets were placeholders that should have been replaced, underscoring the ongoing controversy surrounding AI content in commercial gaming.
