## Block lays off 40% of staff to pivot toward an AI-agent-driven organization

CEO Jack Dorsey announced a massive 40% workforce reduction to restructure Block as an intelligence-first company run by AI agents. This aggressive move aims to eliminate organizational inertia, though it faces skepticism regarding the current maturity of AI technology.
