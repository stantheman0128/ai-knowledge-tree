## Microsoft releases MAI-Transcribe-1 for multilingual speech recognition

Microsoft's new speech-to-text model, MAI-Transcribe-1, supports 25 languages and claims industry-leading accuracy. It is now available via Microsoft Foundry and integrated into Copilot.
