## NVIDIA details the workflow for transitioning AI-powered robots from simulation to production.

NVIDIA has released a guide detailing how developers can leverage simulation environments to train and test AI robots before physical deployment. This workflow emphasizes the importance of synthetic data and virtual testing to accelerate development cycles and improve safety in real-world robotics applications.
