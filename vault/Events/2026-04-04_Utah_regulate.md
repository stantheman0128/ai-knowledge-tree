## Utah authorizes AI systems to prescribe psychiatric medications without physician oversight

Utah has become the second state to allow AI software to prescribe and refill psychiatric medications, aiming to address provider shortages. Critics and researchers warn that 'cognitive surrender'—where users blindly trust AI errors—poses severe risks for clinical psychiatric treatment.
