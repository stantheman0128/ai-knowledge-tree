## OpenAI partners with the Gates Foundation to support disaster response in Asia

OpenAI is collaborating with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation to host workshops for disaster response teams across Asia. This initiative aims to help these organizations effectively integrate AI tools into their operations to improve crisis management and humanitarian efforts.
