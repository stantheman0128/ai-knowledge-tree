## Anthropic outlines four core principles for product management in the AI era

Anthropic's product leadership has published a guide on adapting product management to the rapid evolution of AI models. The principles emphasize short-term experimentation, prioritizing demonstrations over documentation, and maintaining simplicity to avoid technical debt.
