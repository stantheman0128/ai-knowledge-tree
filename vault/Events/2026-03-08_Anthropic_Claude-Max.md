## Anthropic offers free Claude Max to open-source maintainers

Anthropic is providing six months of free access to its Claude Max model to prominent open-source maintainers to integrate its tools into developer workflows. This initiative aims to capture the developer ecosystem by embedding AI tools early in the software development lifecycle.
