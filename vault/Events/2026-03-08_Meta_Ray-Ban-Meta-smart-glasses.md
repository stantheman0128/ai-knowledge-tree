## Meta faces privacy backlash over human review of sensitive smart glasses footage

An investigation revealed that Meta contractors in Kenya have been reviewing intimate footage captured by users of Ray-Ban smart glasses. This highlights a significant regulatory gap regarding how AI companies handle cross-border data processing and user consent for human review pipelines.
