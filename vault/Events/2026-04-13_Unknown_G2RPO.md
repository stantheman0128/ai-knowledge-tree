## G2RPO normalizes reward distributions for improved multi-task reinforcement learning.

G2RPO addresses the challenge of mismatched reward distributions in multi-task RL by normalizing task advantages to a standard normal distribution. This technique allows models to balance diverse tasks like OCR and geometric reasoning more effectively.
