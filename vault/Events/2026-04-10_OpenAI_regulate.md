## Florida Attorney General launches investigation into OpenAI over national security concerns

Florida's attorney general has opened an investigation into OpenAI, citing concerns that the company's technology and data could pose national security risks or harm consumers. This state-level probe highlights a growing trend of independent regulatory action against AI firms, independent of federal legislative progress.
