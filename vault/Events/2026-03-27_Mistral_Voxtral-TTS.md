## Mistral AI releases Voxtral TTS text-to-speech model

Mistral AI has open-sourced Voxtral TTS, a 4-billion parameter model capable of zero-shot voice cloning and cross-lingual speech migration. It supports 9 languages and utilizes a transformer-based flow matching architecture.
