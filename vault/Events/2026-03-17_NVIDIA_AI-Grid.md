## NVIDIA partners with global telecom leaders to deploy distributed AI grids for edge inference

NVIDIA is collaborating with major telecommunications operators like AT&T, Comcast, and Akamai to transform network infrastructure into distributed AI grids. By leveraging edge data centers for AI inference, these companies aim to reduce latency, lower costs per token, and enable real-time AI applications at scale.
