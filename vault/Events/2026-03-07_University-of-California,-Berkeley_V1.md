## Berkeley researchers identify verification as the primary bottleneck for test-time scaling.

The V1 research demonstrates that pairwise comparison of solutions significantly outperforms independent scoring in reasoning tasks. By unifying generation and verification, the model improves Pass@1 accuracy by 10% while optimizing compute usage.
