## NVIDIA partners with energy leaders to transform AI factories into flexible grid assets

NVIDIA has collaborated with Emerald AI and several major energy companies to integrate AI factories into the power grid as flexible, intelligent assets. By utilizing the Vera Rubin DSX reference design, these AI factories can dynamically respond to grid conditions, improving energy efficiency and system reliability while meeting the massive power demands of modern AI infrastructure.
