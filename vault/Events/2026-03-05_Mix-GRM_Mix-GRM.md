## Mix-GRM optimizes reward models by distinguishing between breadth and depth CoT.

The study demonstrates that reward models perform better when they distinguish between breadth CoT for subjective tasks and depth CoT for objective reasoning. This structural approach outperformed existing open-source reward models by 8.2% across five benchmarks.
