## CoT reasoning acts as an implicit memory search engine for factual queries.

A new Google study reveals that Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning allows models to recall facts they cannot access through direct prompting. The reasoning process serves as a compute buffer and a mechanism to prime related facts, though it also risks propagating hallucinations.
