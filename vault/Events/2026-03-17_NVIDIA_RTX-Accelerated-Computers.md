## NVIDIA RTX-accelerated computers now support direct connectivity with Apple Vision Pro

NVIDIA has announced that its RTX-accelerated workstations and PCs can now connect directly to the Apple Vision Pro headset. This integration allows users to experience high-fidelity, GPU-accelerated 3D environments and industrial digital twins within the spatial computing interface of the Vision Pro.
