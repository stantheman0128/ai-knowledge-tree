## RAGEN-2 identifies template collapse in agentic reinforcement learning

Researchers introduced RAGEN-2, which reveals that RL-trained agents can suffer from 'template collapse' where they use fixed reasoning patterns despite appearing to explore normally. The study suggests using mutual information instead of entropy to monitor training health and improve reasoning performance.
