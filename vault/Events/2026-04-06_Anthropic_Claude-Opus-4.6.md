Anthropic researchers used the Claude Code tool and Opus 4.6 model to identify multiple remote vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel, including a critical NFS driver flaw that has existed for 23 years. This highlights the growing capability of AI models in automated security research and vulnerability discovery.
