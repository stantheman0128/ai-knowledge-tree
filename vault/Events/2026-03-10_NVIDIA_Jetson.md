## NVIDIA Jetson platform enables local execution of open-source AI models for edge and robotics applications

NVIDIA is highlighting the capability of its Jetson edge AI platform to run advanced open-source models locally, eliminating the need for cloud connectivity. By supporting models like Gemma, Mistral, and NVIDIA's own Cosmos and GR00T, the platform enables developers and enterprises to build private, low-latency autonomous systems and robotic assistants.
