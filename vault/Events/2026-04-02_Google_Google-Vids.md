## Google Vids introduces free AI-powered video generation capabilities

Google has integrated its latest Lyria 3 and Veo 3.1 models into Google Vids to enable high-quality video generation at no cost. This update enhances the platform's creative capabilities, making advanced AI video tools more accessible to users.
