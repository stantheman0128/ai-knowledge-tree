## OpenAI research highlights ChatGPT's role in closing the wage information gap

OpenAI released new research indicating that Americans use ChatGPT for nearly 3 million daily queries regarding compensation and earnings. This data suggests that AI tools are playing a significant role in helping workers navigate salary transparency and close the wage information gap.
