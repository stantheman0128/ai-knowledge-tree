## MOSI.AI and OpenMOSS release lightweight MOSS-TTS-Nano speech model

The collaboration has resulted in a 0.1B parameter multilingual speech model capable of running on 4-core CPUs without a GPU. It supports 20 languages and is designed for local, lightweight, and real-time applications.
