## Google partners with the Brazilian government to map and protect forests via satellite imagery.

Google is collaborating with the Brazilian government to develop a new satellite imagery map aimed at monitoring and protecting the country's forests. This initiative leverages Google's mapping technology to support environmental conservation efforts and combat deforestation.
