## xAI faces lawsuit over alleged use of Grok to generate child sexual abuse material

xAI is facing a lawsuit following allegations that its Grok AI was used to generate child sexual abuse material (CSAM) from real photographs. The incident highlights the growing legal and reputational risks for AI companies that lack robust safety guardrails against the misuse of their generative models.
