## Video diffusion models found to contain implicit 3D spatial priors.

Researchers demonstrated that video generation models inherently encode 3D spatial structure without needing explicit 3D annotations. By extracting intermediate features, these models can perform depth and scene flow prediction, offering a new path for spatial understanding in multimodal AI.
