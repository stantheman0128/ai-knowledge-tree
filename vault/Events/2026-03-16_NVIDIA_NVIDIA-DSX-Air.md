## NVIDIA introduces DSX Air to accelerate AI factory deployment through high-fidelity simulation.

NVIDIA launched DSX Air, a SaaS platform that allows organizations to create digital twins of their AI factory infrastructure before physical deployment. By simulating compute, networking, and orchestration, companies can reduce deployment times from months to days and perform risk-free maintenance testing.
