## NVIDIA introduces OpenShell to provide secure-by-design runtimes for autonomous AI agents.

NVIDIA has open-sourced OpenShell, a secure runtime designed to isolate autonomous AI agents within sandboxed environments to prevent unauthorized data access or policy overrides. By separating agent behavior from system-level security enforcement, the tool enables enterprises to safely deploy self-evolving agents across various infrastructure stacks.
