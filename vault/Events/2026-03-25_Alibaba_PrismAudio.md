## Alibaba Tongyi Lab releases PrismAudio for video-to-audio generation

Alibaba's Tongyi Lab has introduced PrismAudio, a framework that uses reinforcement learning and chain-of-thought techniques to improve audio-visual synchronization in video generation. The research has been accepted for presentation at ICLR 2026.
