## Google deploys AI for retinal screening in rural Australia with clinical oversight

Google has launched an AI-based health initiative in rural Australia to screen for cardiovascular disease using retinal images. Unlike law enforcement facial recognition, this system requires a clinician to review every flagged result before diagnosis, highlighting the importance of medical ethics and human-in-the-loop protocols.
