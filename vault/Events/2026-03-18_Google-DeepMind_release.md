## Google DeepMind releases a cognitive framework for measuring AGI progress

Google DeepMind has introduced a new cognitive framework designed to measure progress toward artificial general intelligence by focusing on cognitive dimensions rather than task-specific benchmarks. To support this, the company launched a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource the development of evaluation tests from the public. This initiative aims to establish a shared, objective standard for AGI, moving away from marketing-driven definitions.
