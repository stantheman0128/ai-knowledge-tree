## CCTV exposes GEO black market manipulating AI search results

China's CCTV 3·15 gala exposed a 'GEO' industry where service providers use automated systems to inject false information into AI models. This practice, described as 'poisoning,' allows malicious actors to manipulate AI search recommendations and smear competitors.
