## Flexi-UNet optimizes diffusion models by processing high-noise steps at lower resolutions.

Researchers demonstrated that high-noise diffusion steps contain information equivalent to low-resolution thumbnails, making full-resolution processing redundant. Flexi-UNet dynamically adjusts resolution based on information density to save compute.
