## Tencent releases Penguin-VL vision-language model series

Tencent launched the Penguin-VL model series, which uses a Qwen3-based visual encoder to optimize efficiency. The models include 2B and 8B versions and feature a time-redundancy perception strategy for improved long-video understanding.
