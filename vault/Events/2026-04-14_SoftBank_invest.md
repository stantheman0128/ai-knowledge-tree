## Four Japanese firms establish company to develop trillion-parameter AI model

SoftBank, NEC, Sony, and Honda have formed a joint venture to develop a massive 1-trillion parameter multimodal AI model. The project aims to advance 'physical AI' for robotics and manufacturing by 2030.
