## NVIDIA and partners demonstrate power-flexible AI factories to stabilize energy grids

NVIDIA collaborated with Emerald AI, EPRI, and National Grid to showcase how AI data centers can dynamically adjust power consumption during peak demand. By using the Emerald AI Conductor Platform on NVIDIA infrastructure, these factories act as grid shock absorbers, potentially accelerating data center deployment and keeping energy costs stable.
