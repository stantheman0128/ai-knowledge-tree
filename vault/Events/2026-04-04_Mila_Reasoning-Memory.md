## Mila introduces a library of 32 million procedural routines to improve model reasoning.

Mila researchers developed a system that extracts reusable problem-solving routines from reasoning traces, allowing models to avoid reasoning from scratch. By retrieving these procedural entries at inference time, models achieved performance gains of up to 19.2% on benchmarks. This approach shifts the paradigm from pure test-time compute to experience-based retrieval.
