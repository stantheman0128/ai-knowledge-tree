## English Wikipedia bans AI-generated content due to factual inaccuracies

English Wikipedia has formally prohibited the use of AI-generated or AI-rewritten articles. The decision follows repeated issues with fabricated citations and invented claims in AI-submitted content that failed to meet the platform's verification standards.
