## Alibaba releases Qwen3.5 small model series

Alibaba has open-sourced the Qwen3.5 small model series, including 0.8B, 2B, 4B, and 9B variants. These models are designed for edge computing and lightweight agent applications, featuring native multimodal capabilities and support for long contexts.
