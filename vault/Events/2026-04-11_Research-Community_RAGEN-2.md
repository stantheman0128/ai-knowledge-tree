## New research challenges the binary consensus between SFT and RL training

Recent papers demonstrate that supervised finetuning (SFT) can achieve generalization comparable to reinforcement learning under specific conditions, while RL-trained agents can suffer from 'template collapse.' These findings suggest that training method effectiveness is more nuanced than previously assumed.
